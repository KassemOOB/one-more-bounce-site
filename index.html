Privacy Policy for One More Bounce

Effective date: October 1, 2026

One More Bounce is a mobile game developed and published by Kassem. This Privacy Policy explains how the game handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

One More Bounce does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The game does not require you to create an account, sign in, provide your name, email address, phone number, location, or other personal information.

Local Game Data

One More Bounce may store a small amount of game information locally on your device. This may include:

  • Your best score
  • Your sound or mute preference
  • Whether you have already seen the tutorial

This information is stored locally on your device for game functionality and preferences. It is not transmitted to the developer or shared with third parties.

Advertising

One More Bounce does not contain advertising and does not use advertising networks.

Analytics and Tracking

One More Bounce does not use analytics, behavioral tracking, or third-party tracking services.

Internet and Online Services

The game is designed to work offline. One More Bounce does not require an online account, online multiplayer service, cloud storage, or remote server for normal gameplay.

Data Sharing

Because One More Bounce does not collect personal or user data, there is no personal or user data shared with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Children's Privacy

One More Bounce does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

The game does not include accounts, chat, user-generated content, advertising, or mechanisms for users to submit personal information to the developer through the game.

Data Security

One More Bounce does not operate a server that receives personal user information. Game preferences stored locally on your device are subject to the security protections provided by your device and operating system.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if One More Bounce changes in the future, particularly if new features affect how user data is handled.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of One More Bounce, you can contact the developer using the contact email listed on the One More Bounce page on Google Play.
